Why we started

Fashion that fits your wardrobe and your wallet

LIFT started as a small stall of curated pieces and a big frustration: online shopping in Cameroon meant slow delivery, confusing payments, and little trust. We set out to fix all three at once.

Today we're a full marketplace connecting local sellers, independent designers, and everyday shoppers with MTN Mobile Money, Orange Money, and card checkout built in from day one.

  • Curated men's, women's & accessories catalog
  • Mobile-money-first, secure checkout
  • Delivery tracked door-to-door across 30+ cities
